## Findings that change our plan
1. **Primary school (ilkokul, grades 3–4) has no cell content.** The original "ilkokul" target does not apply to the cell module. Grade 3 only introduces microscopic organisms.
2. **Cells enter at grade 5 (Fen) with a strict limit:** organelle names and functions only, no detailed structure. Cells show up again as prior knowledge in grades 8 and 10.
3. **The detailed organelle teaching is grade 9 Biyoloji (BİY.9.2.1).** It does not list organelles individually; the textbook decides. These terms were **not found** in the programs: nucleoid, plasmid, flagellum, pilus, ribosome, lysosome, Golgi (only as an analogy), centrosome, endoplasmic reticulum, vacuole.
4. Wall structure is explicitly out of scope; bacterial energy metabolism and reproduction are not taught at grade 9.

## Levels
- **Temel (grades 5–8):** cell membrane, cytoplasm, nucleus, mitochondrion, chloroplast, vacuole, cell wall. Names and functions, simple wording, no structural detail. Plant vs animal comparison (FB.5.3.1).
- **İleri (grades 9–12):** all organelles in the glossary, structure and function, prokaryote vs eukaryote, bacterial structures.
- The programs do not list most organelles. Each organelle is tied to a kazanım only where the programme mentions it or lists it as prior knowledge, or covers it through the generic wording 'organeller' (FB.5.3.1 names only cell membrane, cytoplasm and nucleus; BİY.9.2.1 does not list organelles individually). Everything beyond that rests on the cited Sources, not on a kazanım.

## Notes from content work
- Vikipedi has no Turkish page for nucleoid (it sits under *Nükleoit*), animal cell or plant cell (both redirect to *Ökaryot*), or rough/smooth ER (one shared *Endoplazmik retikulum* page); those Sources cite the English page or the shared page.
- BİY.9.2.5 puts the structure of the cell wall out of scope at grade 9. Our İleri wall and envelope text describes them anyway, so they are tied to BİY.9.2.1 (organelles) and cited Sources, not claimed as required by BİY.9.2.5. BİY.9.2.5 is therefore kept only on the bacterium cell (the domains of life). Plasmid transfer and similar detail is enrichment beyond the minimum programme and is labelled as İleri detail.

## Decisions (user accepted the proposal as written)
1. Levels are **Temel (5–8)** and **İleri (9–12)**; "ilkokul" is dropped for the cell module.
2. Temel text stays at name and function only, per the grade-5 limit. The 3D models are the same at both levels, so the visuals may show structure; only the text differs. Temel hides the fun fact in the UI because some are beyond grade 5; the size, location and microscope rows stay.
3. Bacterial structures (nucleoid, plasmid, flagellum, pilus) appear at İleri only.
