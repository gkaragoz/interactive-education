# Organelle asset contract (GLB)

Generated by Blender scripts in `tools/blender/` (ADR 0001), consumed by `src/scene`. A test (`tests/assets.test.ts`) enforces this contract.

## Files
`public/models/organelles/<id>.glb` plus `<id>.meta.json`. Ids (kebab-case, equal to `OrganelleInfo.id`):

Eukaryote: `nucleus`, `mitochondrion`, `rough-er`, `smooth-er`, `golgi`, `ribosome` (one small cluster of 6–10 granules), `lysosome`, `centrosome`, `vacuole`, `chloroplast`.
Prokaryote: `nucleoid`, `plasmid`, `flagellum`, `pilus`.
Decor-only: `cytoskeleton` (branching ochre filament network in a ~12 µm cube, instance-friendly) and `vesicle` (cluster of three glossy translucent spheres, one `_gel` material). Decor assets have no texture files (vertex colour only).
Shells (hollow, nested closed surfaces): `membrane-animal`, `membrane-plant`, `wall-plant`, `envelope-bacterium` (membrane + wall + capsule layers as separate meshes).
`ribosome` is reused by the bacterial scene.

## Space and size
Y-up, right-handed, 1 unit = 1 µm with mild exaggeration for legibility (nucleus diameter ≈ 6, mitochondrion ≈ 3.2 × 1.2, chloroplast ≈ 5 × 2.4, golgi stack ≈ 3.5 wide, lysosome ≈ 1.1, centrosome pair ≈ 1.6 long). The asset is centred on its bounding-box centre at the origin.

## Nodes
Root node `<id>`; children `LOD0`, `LOD1`, `LOD2` (the app toggles `.visible`). Names use letters, digits, `_`, `-` only. Mesh names are `<id>-<part>` (e.g. `nucleus-envelope`, `nucleus-nucleolus`); the app treats the whole organelle as one selectable unit.

## Materials
At most 2 per file, identified by name suffix; the app replaces them with shared materials, so authored values only matter as fallbacks:
- `<id>_clay`: opaque, colour comes from **vertex colour `COLOR_0`** (baked colour variation and ambient occlusion), soft satin finish.
- `<id>_gel`: translucent (membranes, vacuole, capsule); vertex colour carries tint and alpha.
UVs: see "Contract v2" below (unwrapped atlas UVs; the v1 cube-projected tiling UVs are gone).

## Geometry rules
- Every mesh is a closed (watertight, manifold) surface so the stencil cutaway cap works; hollow shells are two nested closed surfaces.
- Budgets (triangles): LOD0 ≤ 60 000, LOD1 ≤ 30 % of LOD0, LOD2 ≤ 60 % of LOD1 (LOD2 ≥ 300). Whole eukaryote scene must stay below about 1.2 M triangles at LOD0 with all instances; prefer instancing in the app.
- Smooth normals with the detail in geometry (cristae, cisternae, pores, ridges), not in textures.
- GLB size ≤ 1.5 MB each, meshopt compression, no embedded textures (colour is in vertices).

## meta.json
`{ "id", "tris": {"LOD0","LOD1","LOD2"}, "bboxMin":[x,y,z], "bboxMax":[x,y,z], "boundsRadius", "closed": true, "generator": "<script>", "seed": n }`. The layout solver reads `boundsRadius`.

## Reproducibility
Generators are deterministic (fixed seeds); re-running overwrites outputs byte-identically. They run headless: `blender -b --factory-startup --python tools/blender/make_organelles.py -- IDS=nucleus,golgi`. Use only `bpy`, `bmesh`, `numpy`, `mathutils`.

## Contract v2: unwrapped UVs and baked hand-painted textures
(Generator: `tools/blender/` texture pipeline, see `docs/BLENDER_PIPELINE.md`; verdict in `docs/QUALITY_GATE.md`.)

**Geometry.** Names, nodes, LOD0/1/2 groups, closed meshes and budgets are unchanged. `TEXCOORD_0` is now a proper
non-overlapping atlas **per material** (one atlas for `<id>_clay`, one for `<id>_gel`), all inside 0–1, with at least a 4/1000
gutter between charts, equal texel density inside an atlas (`meta.uv.<mat>.uvPerUnit` = UV per µm) and no stretching beyond
a measured p05..p95 area ratio (`stretchP05/P95` in meta). LOD1/LOD2 reuse the LOD0 atlas (UVs are transferred, so one
texture set serves all three LODs). `COLOR_0` stays as the fallback colour (use it when no textures are loaded).
LOD0 geometry is about 19 % lighter than v1 (the textures carry the surface detail) and every GLB is still <= 1.5 MB.

**Textures.** `public/textures/<id>/<tier>/<material>_<map>.ktx2` with `tier` in `low | medium | high`, material in
`clay | gel`, and maps:
| file | content | colour space |
|---|---|---|
| `clay_albedo.ktx2` | painted albedo (alpha unused, 255) | sRGB |
| `clay_normal.ktx2` | tangent-space normal, +X right, +Y up (OpenGL / glTF convention), no flip needed | linear |
| `clay_orm.ktx2` | R = ambient occlusion, G = roughness, B = metalness (0), A = 255 | linear |
| `gel_albedo.ktx2` | RGBA, **alpha = opacity** (straight alpha) | sRGB (alpha linear) |
| `gel_normal.ktx2`, `gel_orm.ktx2` | as above for the translucent material | linear |
Only the materials present in the GLB have files (`membrane-*` have both `gel` and `clay`; see the GLB material names).
All files are KTX2 / Basis Universal with a full mip chain: albedo = ETC1S, ORM = ETC1S, normals = UASTC (RDO) at
medium and low, ETC1S at the 4096 high tier of hero assets (size budget; see meta `format` per map). Load with three's
`KTX2Loader` (set `texture.flipY = false`, anisotropy 8-16; the maps are already in glTF (v-down) orientation).
Apply: `map = albedo`, `normalMap = normal` (normalScale 1), `aoMap = orm` (R, optional), `roughnessMap = orm` (G),
`roughness = 1`, `metalness = 0`; for gel set `transparent = true` and use the albedo alpha as opacity.

**Resolutions** (square, per tier): heroes (`nucleus`, `wall-plant`, `membrane-animal`, `membrane-plant`,
`envelope-bacterium`, `mitochondrion`, `chloroplast`, `rough-er`) high 4096 / medium 2048 / low 1024; tiny parts
(`plasmid`, `pilus`, `centrosome`, `lysosome`, `ribosome`, `flagellum`) high 1024 / medium 512 / low 256; all other
assets high 2048 / medium 1024 / low 512. A secondary material that covers < 30 % of the asset's surface is stored at half
that resolution (the real size of every map is in `meta.json`).

**meta.json** additionally carries `uv` (per material: `uvMin/uvMax`, `coverage`, `overlap`, `stretchP05/P95`, `uvPerUnit`)
and `textures: { format: "KTX2", path, tiers: { low|medium|high: { bytes, size, maps: [{ name, size, bytes }] } } }`.
Assets without textures (decor) have no `textures` key.

**Budgets** (enforced by `tests/assets.test.ts`): no file >= 25 MB; sum of all tiers <= 120 MB; sum of the medium tier
<= 50 MB.

## Module assets (`public/models/modules/`, `public/textures/modules/`)
Animated learning modules (`docs/MODULES.md`) use the same contract v2 as the organelles (names, LOD0/1/2 groups, closed meshes where
it matters, `<id>_clay` / `<id>_gel` materials, atlas UV0, COLOR_0 fallback, KTX2 tiers `low|medium|high`, `meta.json` with `uv` and
`textures`), with these differences:
- Files: `public/models/modules/<id>.glb` + `<id>.meta.json`, textures `public/textures/modules/<id>/<tier>/<material>_<map>.ktx2`.
- **The author origin is the pivot, the asset is not re-centred.** `meta.pivot` (text), `meta.axes` (text), `meta.units` say what the origin
  and axes mean; `bboxMin/Max` and `boundsRadius` are measured from the pivot.
- **Tinting.** `meta.tintable` / `meta.tintParts`: meshes named `<id>-<part>` listed there carry a NEUTRAL light painted albedo so the app can
  multiply a per-instance colour (maternal / paternal / copy) onto the material of those meshes only; the other parts (e.g. kinetochore discs)
  keep their own colour. Use a cloned material per tint (the albedo is a shared texture).
- Texture sizes (high/medium/low): `chromosome`, `chromosome-single` 2048/1024/512; all other modules assets 1024/512/256 (secondary
  materials half size). Budgets as for organelles, checked separately by `tests/assetsModules.test.ts`.
- Same normal-map note: the GLBs have no TANGENT attribute, so three needs `normalScale.y = -1` on app-built materials.

## Lab batch (v0.9 virtual chemistry lab, `public/models/lab/`)
55 assets in two sub-batches: **A** (vessels, equipment, bench, reagent containers, PPE: 35 assets, sheet `docs/images/lab_v0_9_batchA.png`) and **B** (solids, apparatus,
particle-level props: 20 assets, sheet `docs/images/lab_v0_9_batchB.png`). Same contract v2 as the module assets (`<id>.glb`, `<id>.meta.json`, textures in
`public/textures/lab/<id>/<tier>/`, LOD0/1/2, closed meshes, materials `_clay` / `_gel`), plus the conventions below. World unit = 1 cm for apparatus, Y up, front +Z;
particle props use their own units (atom-ball radius 1). Pivots, axes, tint parts, capacities and anchor points are in each `meta.json`.

- **Glass** is the `glass` mesh in the gel material: real wall thickness (1.0-2.5 mm), rounded rim beads, painted window streaks (a broad and a thin vertical highlight, no view-dependent
  shading is baked), a frosted writing patch and graduation ticks (volume-accurate: ticks are placed from the cavity profile; minor / major lengths). Ticks face +Z.
- **Liquid volume**: vessels also ship a closed mesh `inner` (gel material) that is the cavity just inside the glass, up to the top graduation / shoulder. The app tints it and scales or
  clips it along Y; the asset is not baked as a liquid. `meta.liquid = { mesh, axis, yMin, yMax }` gives the usable range. The Hofmann voltameter has `inner-left`, `inner-right`, `inner-centre`
  (see `meta.liquids`); the thermometer has a red `column` mesh (clay) to scale along Y (`meta.column`).
- **Spout**: beakers and the graduated cylinder have a pouring lip towards +X. Erlenmeyer neck top y = 14 (balloon neck radius 1.7 fits its 1.55 neck).
- **App-textured faces**: `digital-balance` mesh `display` and `flame-mount` mesh `card` are separate meshes in the gel material; `meta.uvRects[part] = [u0, v0, u1, v1]` (glTF texture coordinates, v down)
  is the rectangle of the front face in the gel atlas. Draw the digits / flame into your own texture and map it with that rectangle (or give the mesh its own material).
- **Anchors in meta.json**: `flameMount` / `flameBase` (burner, candle, lighter, flame-mount), `mountPoint` + `buretteOrigin` (burette stand), `holes` (test-tube rack), `plateTop` (hot plate),
  `panCentre` (balance), `ringY` (tripod), `funnelOrigin` (filter cone), `shelfTop` (backdrop), `tile` (bench top 60 x 30 cm; modular along X).
- **Tint**: reagent-bottle caps / labels, powder-jar lid / label, indicator-bottle cap / label, waste-container, lighter body, nitrile gloves, goggles frame, balloon, pH paper strip,
  atom-ball, ion balls, bond-stick, precipitate-crystal, electron-dot have a neutral light albedo (`neutralAlbedo`) to be tinted. Everything else is painted.
- Budgets: every GLB <= 1.5 MB (largest 726 KB), texture totals high 21.7 MB, medium 9.5 MB, low 2.4 MB. Heroes (beakers, erlenmeyer, alcohol burner, bench top, Hofmann voltameter) 2048/1024/512, others 1024/512/256 or smaller.
